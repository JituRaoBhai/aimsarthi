Delete your Tasky account and data

Tasky (also called AimSarthi) · Last updated 26 October 2026

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tasky and log in.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Scroll to About and updates and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

This deletes your login and your online plan, tasks, study sessions, settings and "studying now" signal immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot log in, email [YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL] from the contact email saved on your account (or tell us your username) with the subject "Delete my Tasky account". We will delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, what may remain

  • Deleted: username, login, favourite-number hash, plan, tasks, sessions, test scores, settings, contact email, studying signal.
  • May remain: feedback messages you sent (no plan data), and anonymous daily AI-use counters, which expire on their own.
  • On your phone: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove the local copy.

See also the Privacy Policy.
