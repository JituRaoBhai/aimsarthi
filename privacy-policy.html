We do not show ads, do not sell your data, do not track your location, and do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications: timer alerts, Pomodoro breaks and study reminders. Optional.
  • Persistent storage: stops the phone clearing your saved plan. Optional.
  • The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Who can see your data

Only you can read your plan and sessions. Our security rules block other users from reading them. Service providers that run the app for us (Google Firebase, Google Cloud, Google Gemini) process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept until you delete your account. Feedback messages you sent may be kept after account deletion so we can finish fixing the problem, but they contain no password or plan data. AI usage counters (a number per day) expire with normal clean-up.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data: Profile → About and updates → Delete my account. Details and an email option are on the Delete your data page.
  • Export your data: Profile → Your data → Export backup or Download study sessions.
  • Use without an account: if accounts are switched off, everything stays only on your phone.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password.

Children

AceLife is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, write to [YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL] and we will delete it.

Study and medical content

AceLife is a planning and practice tool. Plans, priorities, topic lists and practice questions are study aids and may contain mistakes, so check them against your official syllabus and standard books. Nothing in the app is medical advice or a guarantee of exam results. AI answers can be wrong. Use "Report a problem or AI answer" in Profile to tell us about anything wrong or offensive.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice in the app.
